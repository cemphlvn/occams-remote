Getting started

  1. Put the phone and the TV or console on the same Wi-Fi.
  2. Open the app, touch the screen once, then tap the pill at the top and pick your device.
  3. For a TV, confirm the code it shows. For a game console, sign in to your console account inside the app, then enter the PIN the console shows in its remote play settings.

The gestures

  • Push the puck to move; tap to select; hold for Home.
  • Swipe in from the left edge for Back.
  • Two fingers up or down for the volume; a two-finger tap to play or pause.
  • Turn the phone sideways for the controller.

Try it without a device

Tap the pill at the top, then Add a device › Demo › Demo TV. Every gesture works on it, and nothing leaves the phone.

A device does not appear

  • Both must be on the same network, and not a guest network.
  • The TV or console must be on, or resting with network access allowed.
  • Allow Local Network for the app: Settings › Privacy & Security › Local Network.
