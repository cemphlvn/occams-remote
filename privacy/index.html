Occam's Remote collects no data. There is no server of ours behind the app, no account to make, and nothing that reports what you do.

What stays on your phone

The devices you pair and the keys they give the app, the console account you link, the controllers you build and your settings. They are stored only on your phone. Deleting the app deletes them.

Where the app connects

  • Your devices, over your own Wi-Fi. The app finds and controls your TV and game console directly on the local network, which is why it asks for Local Network access.
  • Your console maker, only if you link a console account. You sign in on the console maker's own page inside the app. Your password goes to them and never to us; the session they return is kept on your phone and used only to reach your console.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, children included.
